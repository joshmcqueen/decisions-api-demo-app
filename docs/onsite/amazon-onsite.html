Before the role-play · 30 seconds

“I chose Amazon because I’ve spent the last 15 years deploying solutions on AWS. Today I’m focusing on the retail side, where third-party sellers need accurate listings online. I’d like you to play the leaders responsible for the listing experience and catalog review.”

The red bottle and the blue title are the opening example. Either field could be wrong. The question for the meeting is who can resolve the discrepancy and what evidence they need.

Assume a US marketplace pilot. The proposed reviewer workspace is ChatGPT Enterprise. I’ll use my Plus account and synthetic cases to demonstrate Maya’s workflow. All listings, cases and operating assumptions are synthetic.

Amazon · Selling Partner Experience and Listings

Discovery · 3 minutes

“For a seller launching today, it matters whether they are waiting for the page to refresh or for someone to tell them what to fix. Can you break down that time for your last category launch?”

Amazon describes most product information changes as visible within five minutes and some image updates as taking up to 24 hours. A day in the human queue is a hypothesis. Ask for the median and 90th percentile of time to assignment, active handling and time to seller response.

Ask the operations lead which tickets create repeat contacts. Ask Listings who owns the trusted SKU record. If review volume is the constraint, emphasize seller self-service. If evidence gathering dominates, spend longer with Maya.

US truth-in-advertising guidance calls for evidence behind product claims. For the pilot, scope case access by reviewer, agree on retention with security, and have the policy owner and reviewers adjudicate ambiguous cases.

Surface selection · 1 minute

Start with the work people do. An inline editor needs a predictable answer to the same question across many drafts. A specialist needs to ask different follow-ups depending on the evidence. The Decisions API serves the seller. ChatGPT Enterprise lets IT manage who can investigate cases and which tools reviewers may use.

IT connects company sign-in through SAML SSO and uses SCIM to add or remove workspace users through its identity provider. A group role grants catalog reviewers access to the reviewer tool. OpenAI excludes business inputs and outputs from model training by default.

For procurement, Security can check the service scope of OpenAI’s SOC 2 Type 2 report and ISO/IEC 27001, 27017, 27018 and 27701 certifications. The customer reviews its own MCP service and data flows as part of deployment. OpenAI · Business data controls · OpenAI Trust Portal

Seller architecture · 2 minutes

Follow the two inputs in the diagram. The server selects applicable policy questions and gathers the draft and evidence. For the full listing check, one call can return named answers about the title, bullets, image identity, background and overlay. Rules for SKU-specific asset approval run in the application.

The application owns routing. A failed call leaves the check incomplete. Each result belongs to a revision. Clearing a draft check allows the seller to continue in their publishing workflow.

Ask which catalog or asset system the customer would connect first. Measure end-to-end seller wait and cost across edits as well as inference latency.

Reviewer architecture · 3 minutes

The ontology is a versioned mapping from a commercial claim to a source and field. A photo can show the color of the appliance. The manufacturer specification establishes whether it supports espresso. If the field is absent, ask for substantiation.

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) server exposes tools to list assigned cases, open a case and read its evidence. The case-preparation worker uses the Responses API separately from Maya’s conversation in ChatGPT Enterprise. Workspace permissions let her use the tool. The MCP service checks which cases and sources she may read.

The case service verifies authority and current revision at confirmation. It records actor, brief, evidence and policy on the receipt. Security selects the permitted evidence fields and reviews the workspace retention policy with IT. The case-system owner sets a separate retention period for decision receipts. OpenAI encrypts business data in transit and at rest. OpenAI · Business data controls Train reviewers on when to request evidence.

Ask: “Which record would your team trust when a disputed capability can change a seller’s published claim?”

  2. 4–7 min · Reviewer queue. Open Maya’s assigned cases. Briefly show how a repeated merchant submission maps to one case. Open the published coffee claim.

Business case · 1 minute

These are adjustable assumptions. At the same case mix and active time, handling time falling from 20 to 15 minutes corresponds to three versus four cases per active review hour. Measure actual throughput and quality by case mix.

To calculate net value, multiply measured capacity by the loaded rate, identify where it reduces overtime or absorbs demand, then deduct inference, ChatGPT Enterprise seats, integration, quality sampling and support. Track seller wait independently from reviewer handling time.

Ask the sponsor which is worth more for this category: preventing ten referrals or saving five minutes on each remaining investigation.

Pilot decision · 2 minutes

Request timestamped submission, assignment, active work and seller receipt events. Validate active-time instrumentation with a sampled time study. Track shopper reports separately. Match treatment and control by category, seller mix and baseline workload.

Before rollout, agree sample sizes and numeric quality tolerances with the reviewer lead. Include random clears, ambiguous cases, explicit contradictions, missing facts and stale revisions. Re-run labeled cases when a policy, model or ontology changes. In the demo, a candidate image policy was held after it unnecessarily referred an embroidered mark.

IT verifies provisioning and removal for a test reviewer. Security checks that the reviewer can retrieve only assigned cases and signs off on retention. Catalog Operations coaches reviewers using paired examples, provides an escalation channel and audits sampled outcomes. Pause on a breached quality limit, revert the release and adjudicate errors. Add a second category only after its evidence owner validates source coverage and the first cohort meets its gates.

After role-play · 15 minutes

Discuss deterministic checks versus model judgment, the failed missing-evidence evaluation, and which production controls you would test next. Have the customer distinguish gross capacity from realizable cost savings.
